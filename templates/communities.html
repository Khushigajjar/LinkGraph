How DFS works here
1
Pick unvisited node — start a new component, push to explicit stack
2
Explore deeply — pop from stack, visit all unvisited neighbors, push them
3
Mark component — when stack empties, all reachable nodes = one community
4
Repeat — move to next unvisited node until all users are placed
Complexity
DFS traversal: O(V + E)
Skill clustering: O(n²)
Adjacency matrix: O(n²) space
Hub detection: O(n²)
3 different lenses
Components — who is reachable from who
Clusters — who knows similar things
Hubs — who has the widest reach
